Licensing and data notice

Code and prose are provided for research review.

Source speech data

FLEURS: Few-shot Learning Evaluation of Universal Representations of
Speech (Conneau et al., 2022), Hindi hi_in, CC BY 4.0.
<https://huggingface.co/datasets/google/fleurs>

This repository ships no audio and no copy of the FLEURS corpus.
It ships source identifiers, transcripts, derived question/answer
records and acquisition scripts. Reproducing the evaluation requires
obtaining the upstream shard from the publisher and complying with
CC BY 4.0, including attribution.

Model weights

Whisper-small (MIT) and Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct (Apache-2.0) are
referenced by pinned revision hash only. No weights are redistributed.

Derived annotations

Hindi questions, accepted answers and model review labels are
project-generated annotations, not part of FLEURS and not
human-adjudicated. They carry no warranty of correctness.
